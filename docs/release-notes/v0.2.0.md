## Highlights

The game now has a budget, a clock and seasons. You pay for crossings out of a
budget that donations refill each month, buy information about the land before
you build, and watch the year turn: animals come and go with the seasons, a
calendar and time controls (pause, 1x, 2x, 4x) sit on screen, and the game
autosaves each season. Milestones mark your progress along the corridor, and
short first-time hints teach the core loop. Text is larger by default, with four
sizes you can choose from the title screen or in play. It is still Bow Valley
only, and the parts that are not finished are listed below.

## Scope of this release

- **Bow Valley only.** The other eleven regions are authored and appear on the
  world map, but they are locked. The permissions, sub-area unlocks and First
  Nations narrative content (Phase 5) are held behind the cultural-advisor
  review and are not in this build.
- **The Continental Connection capstone is implemented but unreachable**,
  because only Bow Valley is playable (#91).
- **Placeholder art and audio.** The milestone celebration reuses the crossing
  chime. There is no donation sound; a player-configurable one is a later option.

## What's new

**Budget and information (Phase 3)**

- A budget with a starting balance. Crossings cost money, and the budget is saved
  and loaded with the game (#57, #60). The budget and the last donation show on
  the HUD (#61).
- Monthly donations, plus a fundraising trickle when the balance is below the
  cheapest crossing you can buy (#65, #80).
- Each crossing records how much fragmentation it fixes, saved with the game
  (#64).
- Buy habitat, population and corridor information per area (#66), shown in an
  Info panel (#68). Corridor-study hotspots are marked on the map while you place
  a crossing (#74).

**Choosing where to build (Phase 2 completion)**

- The map renders the live world under it at segment zoom (#75), and clicks on a
  segment reach the picker and a confirm panel (#76). The confirm panel shows how
  many crossings already exist there (#105).
- Hovering a dangerous segment highlights it (#84) and shows a connectivity
  label (#107). Hovering a sub-area shows a summary (#106).
- An on-screen toolbar with Add crossing and Info (#90). The map header names the
  focused area and says what to do next (#128, #133).

**Time and seasons (Phase 4)**

- Time controls: pause, 1x, 2x and 4x, from keys and an on-screen widget (#89,
  #108).
- A season clock and calendar on the HUD (#93, #103). Species have seasonal
  presence: the grizzly is absent in winter (#101), and crossing attempts rise in a
  species' migration season (#102).
- Seasonal terrain: rivers freeze in winter and flood their banks in spring
  (#112). Bow Valley has no river tiles, so this does not show there yet.
- The game autosaves on each season change, and the title menu has Continue
  (#110).

**Milestones and hints (Phase 6)**

- A milestone tracker with first-crossing milestones (#91), a track on the HUD,
  and a celebration for each (#111).
- First-time hints that explain the core loop (#92). A new player sees a
  persistent first-step prompt (#131). A hint counts as seen when it is shown
  (#129), and "Got it" works in segment mode (#126).

**Text size and the window**

- Named text sizes and a live scale step (#72). The HUD, Info panel, credits,
  confirm panel, title menu and both minigames use them (#73, #78, #79).
- A text-size setting: a title-screen option and Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- / Ctrl+0 in
  play, saved in a settings file separate from saves (#88).
- Larger text at the 100 percent step (body 25, title 45, small 20) in a
  1680x945 window (#137), with one minimum window size of 1344x756 so body text
  never drops under 20 pixels (#138).

**HUD**

- The HUD is split into three panelled zones with a toast stack for messages,
  hints and cues (#132). The build keys line stays up while build mode is open
  (#135). Migration, crossing success and money have separate colours (#136).

## Bug fixes

Found in the windowed walks before and after the feature freeze (V15 to V19).

- The crossing cue is a fixed 2-second window. Before, the "+N crossed safely"
  cue waited for 2 seconds with no crossing, so at 4x it piled up into counts like
  +427 (#125, ATT-173).
- HUD rows reserve their real height so stacked rows stop overlapping (#127,
  ATT-145).
- The unlocked world-map card no longer draws solid white (#134, ATT-180).
- The build toolbar stays inside the window (#95).
- Snowpack Survey keeps its verdict buttons and tool line on screen at every text
  step (#130, ATT-167). At 175 percent its legend, banner and hardness line no
  longer overlap or clip (#142, ATT-186).
- Signal Chase keeps its antenna readout and signal meter on screen at 175 percent
  (#140, ATT-185).
- The confirm panel stays clear of the HUD panels and the map header at every
  text step (#141, ATT-184).

## Known issues

- **Windows shows a warning, as in v0.1.0.** The Windows build is unsigned. To be
  confirmed at the cut (B9): the signing and checksum steps for this release.
- **Eleven regions are locked** and cannot be entered.
- **The crossing cue text can show twice for a moment** in the toast stack
  (ATT-189).
- **The "Tutorial loaded" log line shows briefly as a toast** above the first-step
  prompt (ATT-183).
- **The Info panel does not refresh while it is open** (ATT-178).
- **Play time is not counted** in the saved game (ATT-179).
- **A hover label can sit under the confirm panel** (ATT-181), and **HUD text is
  faintly visible behind the minigame backdrops** (ATT-182).
- **A v0.1.0 save loads, but its economy starts empty.** Tested with a save written
  by the v0.1.0 build: the crossing, patches and clock all restore and the game
  runs on. That save has no budget, so the budget loads as $0 (a new game starts
  at $50,000); the monthly donations and the fundraising trickle refill it. The
  old crossing has no stored fragmentation deficit, so by the donation
  formula it earns the minimum multiplier until you build new ones (read from
  the code, not watched in play).
- **The smallest window size is not measured in the built game.** It was checked
  by eye on the Mac at 1344x756; the exact size was not read back.
- **Only the Windows binary is boot-tested in CI.** The Mac and Linux builds are
  checked by hand at the cut.
