## Addendum — Phase 1 movement + connectivity (pathfinding, connectivity_graph)

Built the two graphs from simulation-design §1: the fine movement graph and the
coarse patch-adjacency graph.

### What got done

- **`scripts/systems/pathfinding.gd`** (`Pathfinding`) — A* over the hex movement
  graph (simulation-design §2). Edge eligibility/cost from tile flags + crossing
  coverage: uncovered barrier = no edge; covered = zero-mortality at crossing cost;
  uncovered hazard = `HAZARD_AVOIDANCE_MULT` cost and not zero-mortality; plain =
  base. `path_crosses_hazard()` distinguishes a safe route from a risky one.
  Stateless, operates on a `WorldData` grid.
- **`scripts/systems/connectivity_graph.gd`** (`ConnectivityGraph`) — biome-based
  patch derivation (ADR 0007): contiguous same-biome-group terrain = one patch;
  hazards/barriers split habitat. Contiguity links (adjacent terrain) and crossing
  links (`add_safe_link`) form networks; `habitat_tile_count()` gives the
  species-relative effective size (habitat biomes resolved through the alias map),
  and `is_viable()` compares it to `min_viable_patch_size`.
- **Tests:** `tests/pathfinding_test.gd` (impassable blocks, hazard traversable,
  covered barrier, full-vs-partial span zero-mortality, covered preferred) and
  `tests/connectivity_graph_test.gd` (3-patch derivation, hazard separation,
  grizzly rescue, alias resolution).

  `simulation_constants.gd` — referenced throughout simulation-design §2 but
  missing from data-schemas §10 (flagged in-code to reconcile §10 later).
