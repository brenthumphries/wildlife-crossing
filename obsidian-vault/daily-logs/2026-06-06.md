## Decisions made

- **Segment is now defined.** A segment is a pre-defined section of roadway or
  barrier large enough to include all crossing construction tiles plus surrounding
  space for fencing and animal-guidance constructs, and small enough for the
  construction workflow to operate without scope ambiguity. Segment boundaries
  are fixed world data, not adjustable by the player.
- **Y2Y is the entire game map.** The Yukon to Yellowstone corridor is the full
  geographic extent of the game — not a starting region with potential expansion.
- **Geographic navigation is continuous zoom, not discrete menus.** The player
  scrolls and zooms without level-transition screens. This makes location
  selection feel spatial rather than menu-driven.
- **Sub-areas are roughly equal in geographic size.** Exact boundaries, names,
  and count are deferred to the sub-areas PRD, but the equal-size constraint is
  established.
- **Cultural narrative design is a separate document from permission mechanics.**
  The how-it-works (permissions PRD) and the how-it-feels / who-it-involves
  (cultural-narrative-design doc) are kept separate so the mechanical system can
  be iterated on without conflating it with the more sensitive cultural design
  questions.
