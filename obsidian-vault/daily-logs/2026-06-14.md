## Decisions made

- **No new system PRDs.** Detailed specs that lived only as concept paragraphs in
  the overview (habitat, species, economy, seasons) were folded into
  `game-design-overview.md` rather than spun out into separate PRDs — keeps the
  overview as the single anchor doc.
- **Generation requirements belong in `docs/`, not a PRD.** It's a meta-plan for
  producing design docs, so it moved to `docs/design-documentation-plan.md`.
- **Refinements migrate.** Where fable improved an existing section (Vision,
  Pillars, Loop, interactions), the improved wording was carried into the
  overview, not just the net-new content.
- **Open questions get resolved in place.** When a decision migrated, the matching
  open question in the destination PRD was marked resolved (struck + a *Resolved:*
  note), matching the vault's existing convention.
- **Strikethrough at the section-heading level.** Markdown strikethrough doesn't
  render cleanly across tables/code blocks, so migrated fable sections are marked
  by striking the heading and adding a migration pointer, with the full body left
  intact beneath.
