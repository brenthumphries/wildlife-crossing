## What I worked on

- **Not observed by sight:**
  - **River freeze and flood.** Bow Valley has no river tiles, so neither can be seen in this build. They are covered by tests only.
  - **Species on the map.** Every animal is the same white dot. `game/assets/sprites/` holds only the crossing cue and the map image, and the `sprite_set` field in `species_stats.json` is used by no script. Presence was checked through the Info panel's population survey instead.
  - **Migration-season crossing spike.** Not judgeable by eye. The "+N crossed safely" cue is unreliable at 4x (ATT-173).

- **Info panel does not refresh while it is open.** A survey bought late in fall showed the grizzly without "away" and a budget of $44,821 while the HUD behind it said $47,255 and the season had flipped to winter. Closing and reopening the panel showed "away". The game keeps running behind the panel. Not a seasons defect; minor.

- **`playtime_seconds` is 0** in the autosave's `meta` after several minutes of play. It may be a field nothing writes yet. Not checked.

## Decisions made

- **Q13: the milestone track stays top-left, directly under the time controls, with no click-through** (Brent's call). V18 showed it working as built: banner, "Corridor 1/12" and chime. This agrees with ATT-146, which already puts the track under the clock. The spec's bottom-right click-to-open full track is not built and not wanted for v0.2.0. No code item.
- **Q10: the feature freeze starts when the list below is merged and the V15 and V18 re-walks pass** (Brent's call; option 1, with every item kept on the list). This follows the 2026-10-16 rule: features merged and walked, then frozen. The start date is written into a log when the last re-walk passes. After it only fixes and V19 findings merge, until v0.2.0 is cut.
  - **Merge first:** the HUD items ATT-145, ATT-146, ATT-147, ATT-148, ATT-168 and ATT-169; Snowpack Survey ATT-167; the first-play items ATT-172 to ATT-177. About 32 estimate points.
  - **Then walk:** the HUD at each text size and the minimum window (ATT-149), V15 again, and V18 again (ATT-171).
  - ATT-169 waits on ATT-167 and ATT-168, and ATT-146 waits on ATT-145. ATT-174 takes its copy from ATT-170, which is still open.
- **Q11: `SEGMENT_PICK_RADIUS` stays at 1** (`game/scripts/main.gd`), no code item (Brent's call). A click on a tile one step from a dangerous segment picks it. Kept because segment mode starts at 16 px per tile and a trackpad click on a one-tile-wide road is fiddly; the slack helps a new player. The PRD's "plain terrain does nothing" holds for tiles beyond that one step. Whether hover matches the click was not checked.
- Q12 (flooded banks next to a crossing) was not judged: the build has no river to flood. See Open questions.
