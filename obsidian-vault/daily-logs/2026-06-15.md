## What I worked on

Designed an AI-assisted map generation approach for the Crown of the Continent
sub-area, and executed Phase 1 of that approach: ecological research and zone
map design for the 100×100 tile game map.

## What got done

- Defined a four-phase AI map generation pipeline: (1) ecological zone map,
  (2) Midjourney overview reference, (3) individual tile archetype generation,
  (4) manual assembly in Tiled → Godot.
- Selected tools for each phase: Claude for zone planning, Midjourney for
  overview and tile aesthetics, DALL-E 3 for controlled variants, Aseprite
  for pixel art finalization, Tiled for map assembly.
- Completed Phase 1: researched Crown of the Continent ecology (biomes,
  species, corridors) and designed the full zone layout.
- Produced `[[crown-of-continent-zone-map]]` — a design document with nine
  ecological zones, geographic features, four wildlife corridor routes, an
  18-archetype tile list for Phase 3, and a Midjourney prompt brief for
  Phase 2.
- Produced an interactive zone map visualization (100×100 grid, hover to
  identify zones, corridor overlays, Continental Divide marker).

## Decisions made

- **AI generation is for iteration reference, not final assets.** Generated
  images will be used as drafts and references for hand-refined pixel art —
  not imported directly as game tiles.
- **9 ecological zones** defined for the Crown of the Continent map: Glacier,
  Alpine Meadow, Subalpine Forest, West Montane Forest, East Montane Forest,
  Riparian Corridor, Lake/Open Water, Wetland & Meadow, Prairie/Grassland.
- **Geographic accuracy is approximate.** Ecological inspiration takes priority
  over topographic replication.
- **Map is one-time authored** (not procedurally generated).
- Western lake (Lake McDonald analog) and eastern lake (St. Mary analog) are
  the two primary water bodies anchoring the map.

## Open questions / blockers

- Does the map include a human infrastructure layer (roads, fences, crossings)
  from the start, or is that added later as a design layer? This affects Phase
  3 tile archetype scope significantly.
- Canonical tile pixel size not yet decided: 16×16 vs 32×32.
- Seasonal tile variants (especially western larch autumn gold) — in scope for
  Phase 3 or deferred?
- Should in-game water features have real-world-inspired names or fictional ones?
