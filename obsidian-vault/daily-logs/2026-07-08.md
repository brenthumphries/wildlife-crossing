## Decisions made

- **Fragmentation metric (B2)** — nothing pinned it down, so: patch score =
  its network's tile share of all habitat tiles; segment score = mean of
  adjacent patch scores; worst three pulse. Simple, reads correctly in Bow
  Valley; owner review welcome before it calcifies.
- **New `lake` barrier tile (B4)** — the registry had no standing-water tile;
  big lakes (Pend Oreille, Kootenay, Williston, Yellowstone) now split habitat
  as impassable barriers instead of being crossable "river" hazard.
- **`export_presets.cfg` is now tracked** — removed the stock gitignore rule;
  our presets hold no signing credentials (noted inline to re-ignore if
  identities are ever added).
- **Budget gate wired with `min_crossing_cost = 0`** — display-only until the
  Phase 3 economy, per the roadmap's phase assignment.
