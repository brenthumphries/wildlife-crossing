## What got done

- **V1 — CI zero-tests guard.** The GUT run now also writes a JUnit results
  file (`-gjunit_xml_file`), and a new `Guard against a silently-empty suite`
  step fails the build unless at least one `<testcase>` was recorded. This
  closes the silent-green hole where a bad discovery config (wrong `-gdir` or
  suffix) would exit 0. Verified both ways: counts 114 on the real run, fails
  on a synthetic empty XML. `game/gut_results.xml` added to `.gitignore`.
- **Segment records for all 11 new maps.** `data/segments.json` grew from the
  single Bow Valley record to 19. Every new map gets a road segment; the seven
  maps with a cleanly connected river also get a river segment (18 new records).
  Tile lists were extracted from the authored world maps by script, so each span
  is guaranteed on-hazard and hex-connected; labels and connectivity notes were
  written by hand against each region's real geography (US-191 Trappers Point,
  I-15 Monida Pass, US-93 Lost Trail, US-95 Purcell Trench, BC-3 Kootenay, US-2
  Marias Pass, BC-97 Pine Pass, Alaska Hwy/Muskwa, BC-37 Cassiar & Stikine,
  Canol Road, plus Snake/Salmon/Peace/Kechika/Liard/Stikine/Nahanni rivers).
  Bow Valley stays `segments[0]` (the infrastructure test indexes it).
- **Real per-segment picking.** Replaced main.gd's hardcoded demo-segment
  hit-test. `WorldRenderer.coord_at_px` inverts the placeholder projection to a
  tile, and a new pure `scripts/ui/segment_picker.gd` (`SegmentPicker`) resolves
  the tile to a segment — direct hit first, then nearest within one hex step for
  forgiving clicks on 1-wide corridors. Picking is scoped to the loaded
  sub-area, since the placeholder card grid still can't render an unloaded map.
- **Tests.** New `segment_picker_test.gd` (7 tests: tile lookup, sub-area
  filter, nearest-within-radius, closest-of-two), new `world_renderer_test.gd`
  (inverse round-trip + snap-to-tile), and a new `test_segments_schema_and_
  geometry` in `data_validation_test.gd` that enforces unique ids, valid
  `hazard_type`, on-hazard tiles, and single-chain connectivity for every
  segment. **Suite: 15 scripts / 114 tests / 2,733 asserts, all green.**

## Decisions made

- **One road segment per new map, river segment only where the river is a
  single connected component ≥ 8 tiles.** The B4 maps are coarse sketches and
  several rivers are authored as visually-diagonal but hex-disconnected runs; a
  segment spanning those would fail `_chain_connected` and could never be built.
  Rather than ship unbuildable segments, river coverage waits on map refinement.
- **Segments sub-stretched to ~Bow Valley scale (~10 long, full width)** instead
  of spanning a whole corridor, so a crossing doesn't require covering a
  50–70-tile road. Mirrors the existing "sector A" framing of the Bow Valley
  record; future sectors (B, C…) can extend a corridor later.
- **Segment picking scoped to the loaded sub-area.** Honest given the card-grid
  placeholder — the click→tile transform is only meaningful over the rendered
  map. Generalises for free once the in-map renderer lands.
