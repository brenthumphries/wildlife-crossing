## Decisions made

- **Take the repository public now rather than as a step of B6.** Brent's call.
  Three reasons, in order of weight: Actions is unmetered on public repositories
  using standard runners, so it ends the storage problem outright instead of
  deferring it; `export_presets.cfg` currently holds no Apple credentials and
  will hold them after C1, so the pre-flight is as cheap as it will ever be; and
  B5 was already a blocker that three consecutive reviews had left open.
- **Limit any branch ruleset to blocking force pushes and restricting
  deletions.** Two rules that read as standard advice would actively break this
  project. *Require linear history* forbids merge commits, which is exactly the
  strategy B2's acceptance depends on. *Require a pull request* with one or more
  approvals locks out a sole maintainer, who cannot approve his own pull
  request. Requiring status checks is defensible later, at the cost of the
  direct-push-to-`main` path the push runbook documents.
- **Configure branch rules after the merge, not before.** A ruleset added while
  the rerun was in flight could have blocked the merge it was meant to protect.

  the one with a deadline: A4 has an App Store Connect `.p8` arriving that Apple

- **`.gitignore` carries no credential patterns at all.** No `*.p8`, `*.p12`,

  and a `.p8` is a generic private key.
