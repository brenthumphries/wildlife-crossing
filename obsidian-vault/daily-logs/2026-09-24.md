## What got done

- **Duplicate tracking caught and corrected, not a new decision.** B4 was already decided on 2026-09-23 (see that log's Decisions made, and the amendment callout already in [[../build-reviews/2026-09-14-next-build]]: *"Amendment 2026-09-23 — B4's decision is made: one archive per platform."*). Today's Linear issue and chat walkthrough duplicated that work; the conclusion reached today (archive) matches 2026-09-23's exactly, so nothing about the decision itself needed to change.
- **ATT-9 corrected.** Its description now cites the true decision date (2026-09-23) and points at this project's own record as authoritative, rather than implying the decision was made today. Marked Done in Linear.
- **Root cause identified: `bops:daily` doesn't read the plan's linked review note.** `week.json`'s `note` field points at `obsidian-vault/build-reviews/2026-09-14-next-build.md`, which already carried the amendment. `bops:next` reads that note (and its amendments) before reporting an item's state; `bops:daily`'s plan-reading step (§2) only reads the plan file itself. Because this project's convention keeps `status: open` on a plan item until its *implementation* lands, not just its decision, a decision-only item can sit "open" in `week.json` for days after being decided elsewhere. `daily` took that stale-by-design status at face value.
