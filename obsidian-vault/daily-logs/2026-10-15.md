### What I worked on

Walked Q1 (what the next build contains) with Brent, checked whether Phase 3 is buildable from the existing spec and code, and logged the decision in `docs/roadmap.md`.

### What got done

- **Q1 decided and logged.** A "Decision logged (2026-10-15)" block under Phase 3 in `docs/roadmap.md`, in the style of the 2026-09-23 block. It names scope, three decisions made with it, one open question and five first work items.
- **Phase 3 is buildable from the spec.** The economy formula, constants and information table are specified in `obsidian-vault/prd/game-design-overview.md`. `economy_constants.gd` holds the constants, `event_bus.gd` declares `budget_changed`, `donation_received` and `information_purchased`, the save schema already carries `economy` and `information`, and the simulation has a 30-day month.

### Decisions made

- **Phase 3 is the next build; Phase 2 completion follows it. Brent's call, 2026-10-15.** Phase 3 is mostly logic and tests, so most of it can run unattended; Phase 2 completion is mostly rendering and needs a person on screen. Rejected: Phase 2 completion first, and both phases as one build (too large to route as S and M items).
