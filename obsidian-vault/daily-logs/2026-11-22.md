## Decisions made

- **The feature freeze is called, starting 2026-11-22** (Brent, Q10). The rule was: freeze when the freeze list is merged and the V15 and V18 re-walks pass. All three hold. From here only fixes merge; no features.
- **The window floor stays at 1344x756** (Brent, ATT-148). Brent asked what size suits a 13 inch laptop and whether that should be the floor. He kept the existing rule: 100% body text never under 20 px, which gives 1680 x 20 / 25 = 1344 wide and 756 high.
- **The Apache licence appendix stays in credits** (Brent). It prints because the credits screen reproduces each licence in full from `Engine.get_license_info()`, and the appendix is part of the standard Apache-2.0 text. The root CLAUDE.md forbids editing licence text from memory. No item filed.
- **The "crossing selection location" toast stays** (Brent). It shows when M is pressed and is not part of ATT-183.
- **Frozen-ice contrast, the 1680x945 window and the milestone banner as a toast** stand as decided in [[2026-11-18]].
