## Decisions made

- **New text-size baseline (V15 tuning, Brent):** body 25, title 45 and small 20 at the 100% step, replacing 20, 36 and 16 from Q6. This is the old 125% size, which is the smallest step Brent found comfortable on the title menu and the HUD. The steps (100, 125, 150, 175 percent) are unchanged, so the 175% step becomes 44, 79 and 35. Recorded here only: `game/scripts/ui/text_scale.gd` still holds 20, 36 and 16 and is not changed. The larger 175% step will crowd the playfield more, so the HUD layout item below should land with it.
- **ADR 0023 waits until after v0.2.0** (Brent). v0.2.0 ships on the current 2D view layer, the 2026-10-16 scope and the feature freeze are unchanged, and the ADR stays `proposed`. Recorded in a Decision logged block in `docs/roadmap.md` (commit `6aae508`).
- **Escape versus Cancel.** The PRD rules decide it: Escape exits selection mode from any state (rule 13); Cancel returns to segment mode (rule 12). Both behave that way. Claude gave the wrong expected result for Escape on the confirm panel at first and corrected it from the PRD.

## Open questions / blockers

- **Overlay contrast.** The connectivity overlay is visible over the live world but hard to tell its colours apart. Which part is hard (orange against teal, the opacity, or the two states against the terrain) was not recorded. It needs a code item if Brent wants it fixed.
- **Q11 (pick radius).** `SEGMENT_PICK_RADIUS` is 1, so a click on a tile next to a segment picks it. Brent has not decided whether to tighten it.
- **Activation threshold.** Brent read about 11 px per tile at the switch into segment mode; `SEGMENT_ZOOM_ACTIVATE_PX` is 16 and `SEGMENT_ZOOM_DEACTIVATE_PX` is 12 (`simulation_constants.gd:14`). Where the figure was read, and its unit, is unknown. Not treated as a failure.
- **Not checked in the V16 walk:**
  - whether the overlay cleared and the toolbar returned after the Escape from segment mode, though the Escape from the world view showed both working through the same exit
  - build mode showing no hotspot markers without a corridor study (needs a fresh game)
  - pan other than the trackpad scroll: the controller has no drag or key to move the map sideways, and the C25 test's "pan" most likely means the trackpad gesture
  - a mouse wheel, since this Mac has none
- **PR #117.** CI had not reported. The five required checks must report green on the head before `tools/warden merge 117`.
- **V15 is not closed.** Defects found: Snowpack Survey is unplayable at every step (the pit is not fully on screen; no log records it ever being played windowed, so whether it regressed is unknown; the test `test_controls_text_fits_the_viewport_at_every_text_scale_step` checks the controls text, and C22's acceptance asks only that the minigames "remain playable", which this fails); the segment-mode header overlaps the season line at every step; the HUD stack covers the left third of the map at 175%. Each needs a code item. Brent has not said which to raise or in what order.
