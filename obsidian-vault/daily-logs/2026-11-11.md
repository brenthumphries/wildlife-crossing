## Decisions made

- **ADR 0023 waits until after v0.2.0** (Brent). v0.2.0 ships on the current 2D view layer, the 2026-10-16 scope and the feature freeze are unchanged, and the ADR stays `proposed`. Recorded in a Decision logged block in `docs/roadmap.md` (commit `6aae508`).
- **Escape versus Cancel.** The PRD rules decide it: Escape exits selection mode from any state (rule 13); Cancel returns to segment mode (rule 12). Both behave that way. Claude gave the wrong expected result for Escape on the confirm panel at first and corrected it from the PRD.

## Open questions / blockers

- **Overlay contrast.** The connectivity overlay is visible over the live world but hard to tell its colours apart. Which part is hard (orange against teal, the opacity, or the two states against the terrain) was not recorded. It needs a code item if Brent wants it fixed.
- **Q11 (pick radius).** `SEGMENT_PICK_RADIUS` is 1, so a click on a tile next to a segment picks it. Brent has not decided whether to tighten it.
- **Activation threshold.** Brent read about 11 px per tile at the switch into segment mode; `SEGMENT_ZOOM_ACTIVATE_PX` is 16 and `SEGMENT_ZOOM_DEACTIVATE_PX` is 12 (`simulation_constants.gd:14`). Where the figure was read, and its unit, is unknown. Not treated as a failure.
