## What I worked on

Evaluated a proposed change to the progression/permissions system: replacing the
current approach — the player building relationships directly with nine distinct
entities (federal, provincial, territorial, and three First Nations
partnerships) — with interactions routed through a single in-game liaison NPC
(an employee of the player's fictional conservation org) who manages all nine
relationships behind one interface. Assessment weighted **cultural
representation** most heavily, against the principles in
[[cultural-narrative-design]].

## What got done

- Reviewed the two governing PRDs: [[governmental-permissions]] (per-entity Trust
  scores, relationship stages, unlock mechanics) and [[cultural-narrative-design]]
  (representation principles, consultation gate, partnership framing).
- Produced a top-5 pros / top-5 cons assessment of the single-liaison model.
- Wrote it up as a design memo:
  [[liaison-npc-abstraction-memo]] in `obsidian-vault/design/`.

## Decisions made

- **No decision adopted yet** — the memo is an evaluation with a recommendation,
  not a settled choice. Filed in `obsidian-vault/design/` (in-progress design
  thinking) rather than as an ADR, per the `docs/` convention that ADRs are for
  settled decisions.
- **Recommendation: do not adopt the single-liaison model as proposed.** The crux
  is that it routes First Nations partnerships through the same NPC as DOTs and
  federal agencies, collapsing a distinction the current design draws
  deliberately and backgrounding communities that
  [[cultural-narrative-design]] Principle 2 insists be centred.
- **Proposed middle path:** keep a liaison NPC for the government entities (where
  a broker figure fits and is thematically apt), but preserve direct,
  distinctly-voiced partnership interactions for the three First Nations. Captures
  most of the production and cultural-review savings without backgrounding the
  Nations.

## Key points from the assessment

- **Pros** centred on reduced "speaking for" risk (P3), a concentrated
  cultural-review surface, honest modelling of real consultation practice, a
  softer "appease the gatekeeper" feel, and lower production cost.
- **Cons** centred on backgrounding the Nations (violates P2), reviving the
  historical "broker/agent" trope, conflating sovereign Nations with permitting
  agencies, filtering Indigenous agency to a secondhand report, and weakening the
  partnership-not-permission framing.

## Open questions / blockers

- **Product-owner decision pending** on whether to adopt the proposed model, the
  recommended middle path, or keep the current direct-relationship design.
- If the middle path is adopted, [[governmental-permissions]] and
  [[cultural-narrative-design]] would both need updates, and the change is
  significant enough to warrant an ADR at that point.

## Next session

- Await product-owner direction on the liaison-NPC question.
- If adopted, draft the PRD updates (and an ADR) for the chosen approach.

## Session 2 — Pre-implementation P0 review

### What I worked on

Reviewed the entire documentation set to identify the remaining questions that
must be answered before writing P0 code. Scope for "P0" was defined as **through
first playable** — roadmap Phases 1 and 2 (core simulation + overpass validation,
then location selection + sub-areas). Output is a new questions doc; no existing
docs were modified.

### What got done

- Reviewed the full set: the four `CLAUDE.md` files, all six `docs/` technical
  docs (architecture, simulation-design, data-schemas, roadmap, test-plan,
  design-documentation-plan), all five ADRs, the design notes (ui-ux-spec,
  art-direction, audio-design), and the P0/P1 PRDs (game-design-overview,
  wildlife-overpass-crossing, crossing-location-selection, sub-areas).
- Confirmed the `game/` tree is still pre-code (only `.gitkeep`s) — no
  `project.godot`, scripts, scenes, or `data/*.json`.
- Produced **[docs/p0-open-questions.md](../../docs/p0-open-questions.md)**: 10
  hard blockers + 7 cross-document inconsistencies, each tagged with the systems
  it blocks, a suggested default where appropriate, and a dependency-ordered
  resolution path.

### Findings — blockers

- **World terrain layout is unspecified (A1, the biggest gap).** Schemas define a
  tile *registry* and the dangerous segment tiles, but nothing says which tile
  sits at each `[q,r]`. No source exists for the actual map; `architecture`'s
  "static world loaded from `data/`" and its `TileMapLayer` nodes need
  reconciling into one authoring pipeline. Most other blockers depend on this.
- **Patch derivation rule (A2)** — "ecologically compatible terrain" is undefined
  (biome-based vs. species-relative; which biomes merge).
- **Animal spawning/lifecycle (A3)** and whether population `count` maps 1:1 to
  rendered animals.
- **Goal selection with no seasons (A4)** — animals must move and die in Phase 1,
  but the only goal-selection rule references seasonal motivation (Phase 4).
- **Population math (A5)** — carrying capacity, recovery rate, and `DECLINE_STEP`
  are called but never defined.
- **Habitat-quality sub-formulae (A6)** — only the caps exist, not how each term
  is computed.
- **A7–A10** — viable-patch sizes vs. real map scale, Godot minor-version pin
  (`TileMapLayer` needs 4.3+), test-fixture strategy, deferred `GameState` JSON
  shape.

### Findings — inconsistencies

- Scene/script directory paths disagree (`game/CLAUDE.md` vs `architecture`), with
  two colliding `WorldMap.tscn` names (B1).
- Species data: one-file-per-species (game-design-overview) vs single
  `species_stats.json` (schemas + test) (B2).
- Tile size 16×16 (`game/CLAUDE.md`) vs 32×32 hex box (art-direction); zoom
  thresholds are tuned to 32×32 (B3).
- `biome_definitions.json` referenced but never schematized (B4).
- `ecosystem_manager.gd` orphaned — in the systems table but in no design doc (B5;
  carried over from 2026-06-17).
- Overpass sprite variants (forest/grassland/alpine) don't cover all habitat
  biomes (B6).
- Animal scene structure lists a `CollisionShape2D` despite tick-based,
  non-physics movement (B7).

### Decisions made

- None binding. Suggested defaults are recorded in the questions doc, but A1–A6
  are genuine design decisions reserved for the product owner before coding
  begins. No existing PRD or design doc was modified this session.

### Open questions / blockers

- All 17 items in [docs/p0-open-questions.md](../../docs/p0-open-questions.md)
  remain open. Recommended resolution order: **A1** first (unblocks the most),
  then the cheap scaffolding decisions (A8/B1/B2/B3), then the simulation-model
  chain (A2 → A3 → A4 → A5 → A6).
- `ecosystem_manager.gd` reconciliation still outstanding (flagged 2026-06-17).

### Next session

- Get product-owner answers on A1–A6 and B1–B4 — once those land, Phase 1 is
  fully specified and codeable.
- After A1 is decided, author the `game/data/*.json` stubs from
  `docs/data-schemas.md` and stand up `project.godot` + GUT (pending the Godot
  version pin in A8).
