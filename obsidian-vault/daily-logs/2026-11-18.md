- **Disproved ATT-173's first diagnosis.** The issue said the "+N crossed safely" window was counted in real time, so 4x held four times the crossings. Reading `game/scripts/main.gd` showed the cause is a debounce: `_on_animal_crossed` reset the 2-second timer on every crossing, so the cue only fired after 2 seconds with no crossing, and the count piled up (+129, +157, +109, +427 seen). #125 makes it a fixed window. After it, at 4x on day 5, the cue read "+8 crossed safely".
- **ATT-180 cause.** The unlocked card drew solid white because its texture was first loaded inside `_draw_sub_area_card`. Loading it in `setup()` into a cache fixed it in a real windowed render. Why Godot draws white on a first in-draw load was not established.

## Decisions made

- **Hint text stays at body size, small only at 175%** (Brent, ATT-146). The long hint at body size reaches the clock panel at 175% in 720 px. Rejected: small at every step, which shrinks first-play teaching for most players.
- **Card grid sits below the clock panel** (Brent, ATT-168). Rejected: hiding the clock in world view, which loses the pause control while choosing.
- **Map header uses the data name "Central Canadian Rockies"** (Brent, 2026-11-15). "Bow Valley" is the tutorial corridor, not the sub-area. The first-step hint dropped the place name for that reason.
- **Frozen-ice contrast accepted** (Brent, ATT-147). The approved blue, green and amber measure 3.72 to 4.43:1 over frozen river ice, under 4.5:1 and over 3:1. Bow Valley has no river tiles, so it cannot show. Lighter replacements are in PR #136's body for when a map has rivers.
- **Window is 1680x945 content, same window, stretch `canvas_items` with `keep`** (Brent, ATT-169, on a 16 inch MacBook). A bigger window alone adds no layout room under `keep`; only the viewport size does. Measured: 1664x936 is the smallest tested size that passes every fit test at every step, 1600x900 fails only the all-toasts stress case at 175%.
- **ATT-148 rescoped to one floor** (Brent). Per-step minimums add nothing because the layout is fixed in content space. The floor rule: 100% body text never under the old 20 px, so 1680 x 20 / 25 = 1344 wide, 756 high.
- **Milestone banner moves to the toast stack** (Brent). Rejected: one clipped line inside the clock panel. The track row stays under the clock (Q13).
- **ATT-180 joined the freeze list** (Brent), because the white block was on the only unlocked card.
- **Minor findings filed as post-freeze items** (Brent): ATT-181 (hover label under the confirm panel) and ATT-182 (HUD text faintly visible behind minigame overlays).
