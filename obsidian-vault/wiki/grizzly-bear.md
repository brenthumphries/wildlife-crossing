The grizzly bear (*Ursus arctos horribilis*) is a wide-ranging omnivore of the
Y2Y corridor's forests and alpine meadows, and a flagship species for
connectivity conservation.

## In-game

- **Habitat:** forest, alpine meadow · **Range:** very large · **Crossing
  preference:** overpass · **Seasonal pattern:** hibernates (absent in winter) ·
  **Status weight:** ×2 (Vulnerable)

## Real ecology behind the parameters

Grizzlies have some of the largest home ranges of any North American land mammal
— hundreds of square kilometres — which the game models as a *very large* range
and a high `min_viable_patch_size`: small, isolated patches cannot hold a healthy
grizzly population. They forage across elevational gradients (valley to alpine)
seasonally, justifying the forest + alpine-meadow habitat set. Grizzlies
**hibernate** through winter, so they leave the simulation in that season. Their
**overpass** preference reflects real findings from the Bow Valley, where large
mammals readily use vegetated wildlife overpasses. Road and rail mortality and
habitat fragmentation are leading threats, which is why their status weight (×2)
makes recovering them pay well in donations.
