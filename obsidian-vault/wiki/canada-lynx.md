The Canada lynx (*Lynx canadensis*) is a snow-adapted boreal forest cat whose
fortunes rise and fall with its primary prey, the snowshoe hare.

## In-game

- **Habitat:** boreal forest · **Range:** medium · **Crossing preference:**
  underpass · **Seasonal pattern:** active all year · **Status weight:** ×2
  (Vulnerable)

## Real ecology behind the parameters

Lynx are **boreal-forest specialists** with large paws that act as snowshoes,
tying them to deep-snow conifer habitat — hence the narrow habitat set. Their
populations track the well-known **snowshoe hare cycle**; this specialist
dependence (rather than body size) is why their range is modelled as *medium*
with a moderate viable patch size. They are active year-round. Lynx, like many
forest carnivores, will use **underpasses**, encoded as their crossing
preference. Habitat loss and fragmentation give them a ×2 vulnerable weight.
