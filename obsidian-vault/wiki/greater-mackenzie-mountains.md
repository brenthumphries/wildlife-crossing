The Greater Mackenzie Mountains form the northern terminus of the Y2Y corridor,
spanning the Mackenzie and Selwyn Mountains across the Yukon and Northwest
Territories (YT/NWT).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 12** · **Controlling entity:** territorial government
  (`territorial_gov`) · starts **locked**; the northern endpoint of the capstone
  **Continental Connection** chain.
- Remote, intact, and lightly roaded; the territorial steward rewards durable,
  sustained stewardship.

## Acknowledgment

The Mackenzie and Selwyn ranges lie within the traditional territories of real
Indigenous peoples; established names of Indigenous origin carry acknowledgment
notes reviewed by cultural advisors (see [[sub-areas]]).

## Real-world basis

The Mackenzie Mountains are among the wildest, most intact mountain landscapes in
North America and form the northern anchor of the Yellowstone-to-Yukon vision —
the destination the capstone milestone connects back to Greater Yellowstone.
