Mountain caribou are the deep-snow ecotype of woodland caribou (*Rangifer
tarandus caribou*), an endangered icon of the inland temperate rainforest and
old-growth ranges of the central and northern Y2Y corridor.

## In-game

- **Habitat:** old-growth forest, alpine · **Range:** very large · **Crossing
  preference:** corridor · **Seasonal pattern:** elevational migration ·
  **Status weight:** ×3 (Endangered)

## Real ecology behind the parameters

Mountain caribou depend on **old-growth forests**, where they feed on arboreal
lichens that take many decades to establish — making them acutely sensitive to
logging and habitat loss, and justifying the game's high `min_viable_patch_size`
and **×3 endangered** weight (recovering them pays the most). They perform
**elevational migrations**, wintering in high snowpack forests and moving with the
seasons. They prefer broad, contiguous **corridors** of intact habitat over
narrow structures, encoded as a corridor crossing preference — a type not yet
available in v1, by design making caribou harder to support until corridors ship.
