Elk (*Cervus canadensis*) are large, gregarious deer of grasslands and forest
edges, among the most numerous large herbivores in the Y2Y corridor.

## In-game

- **Habitat:** grassland, forest edge · **Range:** large · **Crossing
  preference:** overpass · **Seasonal pattern:** strong seasonal migration ·
  **Status weight:** ×1 (Common)

## Real ecology behind the parameters

Elk undertake some of the longest land-mammal **migrations** in the lower
contiguous ranges, moving between high summer range and low winter range. The
game models this as a strong seasonal migration: elk appear and move differently
by season, and crossings built before a migration season see a usage spike. Elk
favour the **grassland/forest-edge** mosaic where forage and cover meet. They
readily use **overpasses**, as documented in the Bow Valley. As a common,
abundant species their status weight is ×1 — recovering elk pays modestly, but
their high numbers make them a frequent, visible early-game crossing user.
