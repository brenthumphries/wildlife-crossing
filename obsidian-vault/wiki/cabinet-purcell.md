The Cabinet–Purcell sub-area spans the Cabinet Mountains, the Purcell Trench, and
the Highway 95 corridor where Montana, Idaho, and British Columbia meet
(MT/ID/BC).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 4** · **Controlling entity:** state/provincial transport
  authorities (`transport_authorities`) · starts **locked**.
- A corridor region where **highway crossings** are the decisive tool, valued by
  transport authorities who reward completed infrastructure and mortality
  reduction.

## Real-world basis

The Cabinet–Purcell is a crucial but fragile **transboundary linkage** for a small
and isolated grizzly population, pinched by highways and human development. It is
a focus of cross-border connectivity work in the Yellowstone to Yukon vision.
