The pronghorn (*Antilocapra americana*) is North America's fastest land animal
and the sole survivor of an ancient endemic family, ranging the sagebrush and
grasslands of the southern Y2Y corridor.

## In-game

- **Habitat:** grassland, sagebrush · **Range:** large · **Crossing
  preference:** overpass (will not use underpasses) · **Seasonal pattern:**
  strong seasonal migration · **Status weight:** ×1 (Common)

## Real ecology behind the parameters

Pronghorn make one of the longest large-mammal migrations in North America (the
"Path of the Pronghorn" near Greater Yellowstone). Crucially, pronghorn are
**reluctant to use underpasses or go beneath structures** — they evolved to
detect predators across open country and avoid enclosed spaces — so the game
encodes a hard preference for **overpasses** and a refusal of underpasses. This
makes them a deliberate design lever: in v1 (overpasses only) they are well
served, but they illustrate why crossing *type* matters. Their open-country
habitat (grassland, sagebrush) and migratory pattern drive their large range and
seasonal presence.
