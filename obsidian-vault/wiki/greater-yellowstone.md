Greater Yellowstone is the southernmost sub-area of the Y2Y corridor, anchored by
Yellowstone and Grand Teton National Parks and the Wind River Range (WY/MT/ID).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 1** · **Controlling entity:** U.S. Federal Lands Agency
  (`us_federal_lands`) · starts **locked**.
- Home range of [[pronghorn]] and [[elk]] migrations; a marquee region for the
  southern end of the [[connectivity|corridor]].

## Real-world basis

The Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem is one of the largest nearly-intact temperate
ecosystems on Earth and the southern anchor of the Yellowstone-to-Yukon vision.
It hosts the famous **"Path of the Pronghorn"** migration and the wolf
reintroduction that demonstrated a landscape-scale trophic cascade.
