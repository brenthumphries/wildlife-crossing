The Crown of the Continent centres on Glacier and Waterton Lakes National Parks
and the Flathead Valley, where Pacific-moist, prairie, and alpine ecosystems
converge (MT/BC/AB).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 6** · **Controlling entity:** Ksanka Confederacy *(fictional First
  Nations partnership)* (`ksanka_confederacy`) · starts **locked**; designed as
  the **first unlock** with a low trust threshold, introducing the partnership
  mechanic early.
- One of the most ecologically complete temperate landscapes in North America;
  see the detailed [[crown-of-continent-zone-map]] for its ecological zones.

## Acknowledgment

The Crown of the Continent is the traditional territory of real Indigenous
Nations. The in-game Ksanka Confederacy is **fictional**, created to honour
rather than represent those Nations; all related content passes cultural-advisor
review (see [[narrative-content-plan]]).

## Real-world basis

The Crown of the Continent Ecosystem spans roughly 16,000 square miles of
largely intact wilderness and is a UNESCO-recognised region of exceptional
biodiversity — a keystone of the Y2Y corridor.
