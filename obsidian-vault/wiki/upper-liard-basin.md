The Upper Liard Basin spans the Liard Plateau and Cassiar Mountains where British
Columbia meets the Yukon (BC/YT).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 10** · **Controlling entity:** territorial government
  (`territorial_gov`) · starts **locked**.
- A northern, lightly developed region where the territorial steward values
  **sustained stewardship** — active crossings and population stability over time.

## Acknowledgment

The name *Liard* and this region overlap the traditional territories of real
Indigenous peoples; established names of Indigenous origin carry acknowledgment
notes reviewed by cultural advisors (see [[sub-areas]]).

## Real-world basis

The Liard watershed forms a major northern transition in the Y2Y corridor,
linking the central Rockies to the Mackenzie ranges. Low road density keeps much
of it permeable — connectivity to defend rather than restore.
