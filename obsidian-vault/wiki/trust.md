**Trust** is the progression currency of Wildlife Crossing: a per-entity measure
of how much a governing body believes in the player's stewardship. Earning trust
unlocks new sub-areas.

## In-game

Each of the nine governing entities has a **Trust score (0–100)**, fed by
weighted, measurable metrics that differ by entity — crossing usage and road
mortality reduction for federal land agencies; infrastructure completed for
transport authorities; sustained stewardship for territorial governments;
demonstrated ecological outcomes and respect for First Nations partnerships.
Progress shows as four **relationship stages** — Introduced → Engaged → Trusted →
Partnered — plus a checklist of the entity's top three priorities with progress
bars. Reaching the threshold unlocks that entity's sub-area, plays a short
narrative beat, and grants a one-time donation bonus. **Trust is never revoked**;
neglect only slows future trust growth with other entities — a natural
consequence, never a punishment.

## Real-world basis

Real conservation depends on **relationships and consent** among agencies,
landowners, and — first and foremost — Indigenous Nations whose territories the
work crosses. Modelling permission as earned trust, rather than a purchase,
reflects that connectivity work is as much about partnership as engineering.
