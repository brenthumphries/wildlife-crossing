The High Divide links the Greater Yellowstone ecosystem to the wildlands of
central Idaho and Montana, across the Centennial Mountains and Big Hole Valley
(MT/ID).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 2** · **Controlling entity:** multi-stakeholder ranching coalition
  (`ranching_coalition`) · starts **locked**.
- A working-lands region where the player earns trust from a community that
  shares the landscape with wildlife — stewardship that respects ranching.

## Real-world basis

The High Divide is a critical, low-elevation **connective link** between Greater
Yellowstone and the Salmon–Selway wildlands; much of it is working ranchland,
making collaborative conservation with landowners essential. It is a priority
region for the Yellowstone to Yukon initiative.
