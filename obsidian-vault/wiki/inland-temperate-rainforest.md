The Inland Temperate Rainforest sub-area covers the Columbia Mountains, the
Selkirks, and Kootenay Lake — a globally rare wet-belt forest far from the coast
(BC).

## In-game

- **Sub-area 5** · **Controlling entity:** Provincial government, BC
  (`bc_provincial`) · starts **locked**.
- Core habitat for endangered [[mountain-caribou]] and [[canada-lynx]]; old-growth
  connectivity is the regional priority.

## Real-world basis

The interior wet-belt of British Columbia is the world's only **inland temperate
rainforest**, sustained by deep snowpack and supporting old-growth-dependent
mountain caribou. Logging and roads fragment it, making connectivity and
old-growth protection critical.
