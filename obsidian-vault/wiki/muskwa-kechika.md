The Muskwa–Kechika Management Area is a vast, largely roadless mountain wilderness
in northern British Columbia, named from the Muskwa Ranges and Kechika Basin.

## In-game

- **Sub-area 9** · **Controlling entity:** Tāłtsē Dena Council *(fictional First
  Nations partnership)* (`taltse_dena_council`) · starts **locked**.
- Stronghold for [[wolverine]], caribou, and large predator–prey systems where
  large-landscape connectivity remains intact.

## Acknowledgment

The names *Muskwa* and *Kechika* derive from Indigenous languages, and the region
overlaps the traditional territories of real First Nations. The in-game Tāłtsē
Dena Council is **fictional**; the name and all related content are finalised
through cultural-advisor review (see [[narrative-content-plan]]).

## Real-world basis

The Muskwa–Kechika is one of the largest intact wilderness areas in North America
— roughly 6.4 million hectares — and one of the continent's last great
predator–prey systems, a northern anchor of the Y2Y corridor.
