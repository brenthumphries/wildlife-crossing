Region

Central Canadian Rockies

The Central Canadian Rockies sub-area covers Banff, Jasper, Yoho, and Kootenay National Parks and the Bow Valley (AB/BC) — and is where Wildlife Crossing begins.

In-game

  • Sub-area 7 · Controlling entity: Canadian Federal Parks Agency (ca_federal_parks) · starts UNLOCKED (the only sub-area open at game start).
  • The tutorial region. The Bow Valley's real wildlife overpasses are the most studied in the world, so the player begins where crossings demonstrably work and learns the loop with a true story to tell.

Real-world basis

Banff National Park's wildlife crossings along the Trans-Canada Highway — a network of overpasses and underpasses with extensive fencing — have dramatically cut wildlife-vehicle collisions and are documented to be used by grizzly-bear, elk, wolves, and more. They are the real-world proof of concept behind the entire game.
