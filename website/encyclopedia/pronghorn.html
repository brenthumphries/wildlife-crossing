Real ecology behind the parameters

Pronghorn make one of the longest large-mammal migrations in North America (the "Path of the Pronghorn" near Greater Yellowstone). Crucially, pronghorn are reluctant to use underpasses or go beneath structures — they evolved to detect predators across open country and avoid enclosed spaces — so the game encodes a hard preference for overpasses and a refusal of underpasses. This makes them a deliberate design lever: in v1 (overpasses only) they are well served, but they illustrate why crossing type matters. Their open-country habitat (grassland, sagebrush) and migratory pattern drive their large range and seasonal presence.
