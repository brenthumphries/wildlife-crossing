Stikine–Nass–Skeena Headwaters

The Stikine–Nass–Skeena Headwaters — the "Sacred Headwaters" — is the shared birthplace of three major salmon rivers, on the Spatsizi Plateau of northern British Columbia.

In-game

  • Sub-area 11 · Controlling entity: Three Rivers Nations (fictional First Nations partnership) (three_rivers_nations) · starts locked.
  • A late-corridor region foregrounding watershed connectivity and the link between healthy corridors and salmon-bearing rivers.
Acknowledgment

The names Stikine, Nass, and Skeena are of Indigenous origin, and the Sacred Headwaters lie within the traditional territories of real First Nations. The in-game Three Rivers Nations is fictional; the name and related content are finalised through cultural-advisor review (see narrative content plan).

Real-world basis

The Sacred Headwaters give rise to the Stikine, Nass, and Skeena rivers from a single alpine plateau — a globally significant salmon stronghold and a focus of Indigenous-led stewardship in the northern Y2Y corridor.
