Peace River Break

The Peace River Break is a low-elevation pinch point in the corridor, where the Hart Ranges, the Peace River canyon, and Highway 97 constrict wildlife movement (BC).

In-game

  • Sub-area 8 · Controlling entity: Provincial government, BC (bc_provincial) · starts locked.
  • Geographically narrow, so its playable map is denser than others (a documented size deviation under the equal-playable-size rule).

Real-world basis

The Peace River Break is the most significant natural and human-caused constriction in the Y2Y corridor — a low, developed valley that animals must cross to move between the southern and northern Rockies. Keeping it permeable is a top connectivity priority.
