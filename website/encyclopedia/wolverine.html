Wolverine

Gulo gulo

The wolverine is a powerful, far-ranging mustelid of high alpine and subalpine country, one of the most connectivity-dependent species in the Y2Y corridor.

Real ecology behind the parameters

Wolverines maintain enormous home ranges — individuals can cover hundreds of square kilometres — captured as a very large range and a high viable patch size. They depend on persistent spring snowpack for denning, tying them to alpine/subalpine habitat and making them sensitive to a warming climate. They do not hibernate or migrate, so they are active all year in the simulation. Wolverines need large, intact, connected landscapes and avoid heavily modified terrain, encoded as a corridor crossing preference. Their vulnerability and low densities give them a ×2 status weight.
