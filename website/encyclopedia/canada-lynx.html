Canada Lynx

Lynx canadensis

The Canada lynx is a snow-adapted boreal forest cat whose fortunes rise and fall with its primary prey, the snowshoe hare.

Real ecology behind the parameters

Lynx are boreal-forest specialists with large paws that act as snowshoes, tying them to deep-snow conifer habitat — hence the narrow habitat set. Their populations track the well-known snowshoe hare cycle; this specialist dependence (rather than body size) is why their range is modelled as medium with a moderate viable patch size. They are active year-round. Lynx, like many forest carnivores, will use underpasses, encoded as their crossing preference. Habitat loss and fragmentation give them a ×2 vulnerable weight.
