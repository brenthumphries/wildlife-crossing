Salmon–Selway–Bitterroot

The Salmon–Selway–Bitterroot sub-area encompasses some of the largest wilderness in the contiguous United States, including the Frank Church–River of No Return Wilderness and the Bitterroot Range (ID/MT).

In-game

  • Sub-area 3 · Controlling entity: U.S. Federal Lands Agency (us_federal_lands) · starts locked.
  • Rugged, lightly roaded country — strong habitat for gray-wolf and grizzly-bear where connectivity is maintained.

Real-world basis

This complex of wilderness areas forms a vast core of intact habitat in central Idaho, vital to wolf and grizzly recovery and to the southern Y2Y corridor's connectivity. Highway corridors at its edges are key fragmentation points.
