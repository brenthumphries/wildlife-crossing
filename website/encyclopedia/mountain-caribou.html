Mountain Caribou

Rangifer tarandus caribou

Mountain caribou are the deep-snow ecotype of woodland caribou, an endangered icon of the inland temperate rainforest and old-growth ranges of the central and northern Y2Y corridor.

Real ecology behind the parameters

Mountain caribou depend on old-growth forests, where they feed on arboreal lichens that take many decades to establish — making them acutely sensitive to logging and habitat loss, and justifying the game's high min_viable_patch_size and ×3 endangered weight (recovering them pays the most). They perform elevational migrations, wintering in high snowpack forests and moving with the seasons. They prefer broad, contiguous corridors of intact habitat over narrow structures, encoded as a corridor crossing preference — a type not yet available in v1, by design making caribou harder to support until corridors ship.
