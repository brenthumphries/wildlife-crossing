Grizzly Bear

Ursus arctos horribilis

The grizzly bear is a wide-ranging omnivore of the Y2Y corridor's forests and alpine meadows, and a flagship species for connectivity conservation.

Real ecology behind the parameters

Grizzlies have some of the largest home ranges of any North American land mammal — hundreds of square kilometres — which the game models as a very large range and a high min_viable_patch_size: small, isolated patches cannot hold a healthy grizzly population. They forage across elevational gradients (valley to alpine) seasonally, justifying the forest + alpine-meadow habitat set. Grizzlies hibernate through winter, so they leave the simulation in that season. Their overpass preference reflects real findings from the Bow Valley, where large mammals readily use vegetated wildlife overpasses. Road and rail mortality and habitat fragmentation are leading threats, which is why their status weight (×2) makes recovering them pay well in donations.
